# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in Glow Material.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and abusive or threatening conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports may be sent privately to repository maintainers through GitHub and will be handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.
