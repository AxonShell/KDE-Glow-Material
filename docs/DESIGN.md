# Glow Material design principles

## Identity

Glow Material is the MuliOS visual language for KDE. Light is a functional signal: controls illuminate when hovered, focused, selected, or activated.

It is not glassmorphism, a permanently neon interface, or a copy of another platform's material system.

## Principles

1. **Dark surfaces first.** Use deep charcoal with enough tonal separation between content, containers, and overlays.
2. **Light communicates state.** Resting controls stay quiet. Hover, keyboard focus, selection, and active-window states may introduce a soft accent halo.
3. **Soft boundaries.** Use subtle outlines and restrained shadows. Window outlines should be thin and never compete with content.
4. **Purposeful motion.** Transitions clarify state changes; avoid perpetual pulsing and unnecessary movement.
5. **Cross-component consistency.** Plasma, Aurorae, KWin, and Qt integrations share semantic roles while respecting their technical differences.
6. **User control.** Accent color, glow strength, animation, and transparency should be adjustable.

## Initial MuliOS defaults

Deep dark surfaces, MuliOS emerald accent, subtle contextual glow, minimal selective transparency, and short transitions. These are defaults, not hard-coded limitations.
